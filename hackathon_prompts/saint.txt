We're looking for the coolest thing built with a Saint AI agent. Saint is an AI colleague that works inside real email, calendars, documents, browsers and terminals. You give it a job in plain English and it gets on with it, then tells you what it did and asks before anything risky.

Teams going for this bounty get their own Saint AI agent for the whole hackathon. Only 10 teams get one, set up on Saturday morning, so tell us in the Friday 6pm check-in if you're in. Only sign up if you're serious about building with it.
